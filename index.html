📖 القسم الأول: النص القرائي وأسئلة الاستيعاب (Reading Passage & Questions)
"For my job, I am usually on a computer. I design whole websites or just small parts of websites, like menus. I work for different customers. I talk to my customers a lot because I need to understand what they want from their website. I have a lot of online meetings with customers from other countries. Before I start a new project, we have a meeting. We discuss the customer's ideas, and I tell them if they work. Later, we have another meeting. I show them my progress. Sometimes the customer asks for changes. After I finish the website, we have a final meeting. I love my job because I always learn a lot about the businesses. For example, I make websites for several gyms, so I understand a lot about how they work – but I don't go to the gym much. I like doing exercise outside."
